What is included in sales growth & account management

  • Weekly review of orders, price position, stock and buy-box status
  • Deal, coupon and festive-sale nominations planned ahead of each event
  • Inventory planning so you do not run out of your best sellers
  • Account health checks and fixes for suppressed listings and policy warnings
  • Returns and RTO analysis with product page and packaging fixes
  • A single weekly sheet showing what changed and what to do next

Who this is for

  • Sellers with live accounts and flat or falling sales
  • Owners who cannot watch the dashboard every day
  • Sellers preparing for a festive or wedding-season peak

Questions

Questions about sales growth & account management

Can I hire you only for account management?

Yes. You can take account management alone, or combine it with listings, A+ content and ads.

Will you need access to my account?

We work through the marketplace's own user-permission feature, so you stay the owner and can remove our access whenever you choose.

What if my account is already suspended?

We help you understand the notice and prepare a plan of action. Reinstatement is decided by the marketplace, so we cannot promise an outcome.
