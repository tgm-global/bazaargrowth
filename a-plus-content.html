Questions about a+ content & brand stores

Do I need brand registration for A+ content?

On most marketplaces, yes. If your brand is not registered yet, our brand registry service can start that process first.

How many products should get A+ content first?

Start with your top sellers and the products that receive ad traffic. Better pages make every ad rupee work harder.

Can you redo content that was rejected?

Yes. We read the rejection reason, correct the module and resubmit.
