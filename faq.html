FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our services, pricing, accounts and how we work with sellers.

  1. Home
  2. / FAQ

What does Budget Bazaar do?

▼

We provide e-commerce seller services in India: marketplace account setup, product listing and catalogue management, GST and compliance guidance, advertising, account-health protection and product imagery.

Which marketplaces do you support?

▼

We work with Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, beauty platforms, JioMart and other Indian marketplaces. Policies vary, so we confirm the current rules for each channel.

Are you affiliated with Amazon, Flipkart or any marketplace?

▼

No. We are an independent service provider. Marketplace names and logos belong to their respective owners. See our disclaimer for details.

Do you guarantee sales, rankings or account reinstatement?

▼

No. Outcomes depend on your product, pricing, competition and marketplace decisions. We guarantee careful work, clear communication and honest reporting.

How are your services priced?

▼

We quote based on the scope: number of SKUs, marketplaces and services needed. See our pricing page for how quotes work, then contact us for a tailored estimate.

Can I start with just one service?

▼

Yes. Many clients start with account setup or a listing audit and add other services later.

Do I keep ownership of my seller account?

▼

Always. Your account stays in your name with your login. We work through permissions you control and you can revoke access at any time.

How do I contact you quickly?

▼

WhatsApp is the fastest route. You can also call us or use the contact form.

Do you work with sellers outside the listed cities?

▼

Yes. The city pages show where many of our sellers are based, but we work with sellers across India remotely.
