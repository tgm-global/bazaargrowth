Questions

Questions about marketplace ads management

How much should I spend on ads each month?

It depends on margin, price and category competition. Use the planner on this page to see the break-even ACoS and expected orders at different budgets, then we refine it after the first two weeks of data.

Do you guarantee sales or a fixed ACoS?

No one can guarantee marketplace results. We guarantee a clear method, weekly reporting and changes based on your actual numbers.

Who pays for the ads?

Ad spend is billed by the marketplace to your own seller account. Our fee is separate and agreed in advance.
