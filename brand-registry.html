Questions

Questions about brand registry & category approvals

Do I need a registered trademark?

Requirements differ by marketplace. Many accept a pending application. For the legal side of trademark filing, speak to a qualified trademark attorney. We prepare the marketplace side.

How long does approval take?

Marketplace review times change. We keep tracking the application and respond to questions the same day.

What if the application is rejected?

We read the reason, correct the documents and resubmit.
