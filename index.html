Questions

Things sellers ask before they start

Which marketplaces do you work with?

We work with Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Nykaa, Ajio, JioMart and ONDC, and we also build your own Shopify or WooCommerce store. We recommend the mix that suits your category and margin.

Who owns the seller accounts?

You do. Accounts are opened in your business name with your documents. We work through permissions you control and you can remove our access at any time.

Can you guarantee sales or a top ranking?

No one can, because marketplaces decide rankings. We control listing quality, ad structure and response speed, and we report the numbers weekly so you can judge results yourself.

I already have an account. Can you help only with ads or A+ content?

Yes. You can take one service or the whole chain, and add more later.

Do you work with sellers outside the metro cities?

Yes. Everything runs on calls, WhatsApp and shared sheets. You need courier pickup at your location, and we help you check that.

How do I get started?

Send the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp. We book a short free audit call, review your products and accounts, and propose a plan.
