Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, Bazar Growth collects the details you enter: your name, mobile number, city, the marketplaces you are interested in and a description of your products. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we keep that conversation to serve you.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, book an audit call, prepare a plan and provide the services you request. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing and third parties

Enquiry forms may be processed by Web3Forms, and messages may pass through WhatsApp. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling from the Tailwind CDN, which may receive your IP address and browser details under their own policies.

Seller account information

If you become a client, we may handle business documents such as GST, PAN and bank details to open accounts. We use them only for that purpose and keep them secure. Accounts stay in your name.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to provide services or meet legal duties. You may ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies. Third-party services listed above may use their own technical storage.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Questions? Contact us. See also our privacy policy, terms of service and disclaimer, or return to the home page.
